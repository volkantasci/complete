Attendances Timeline

The attendances of the employees on a timeline, one row per employee, and what you can do on it: open, create, move and correct attendances. The module installs by itself when the Attendances application and the Employees timeline module are both present, and adds no menu, group or setting.

  • Attendances > Management > Dashboard opens the Gantt view first, on the Day range of today, grouped by employee. My Attendances offers it as its second view, after the calendar, with a row for your own employee.
  • Every employee has a row, also without attendances, with the avatar and the employee card, and a label and bar of the hours worked against the hours expected. Open attendances run up to now. Cells outside contracts and working hours are grey, columns nobody works fold, and a name is red when the employee has no contract in the period.
  • A click on an attendance opens it at once in a dialog; a click on an empty cell, a drag over cells or the New button create one. On the week, month and year scales the check-out is proposed as the check-in plus the employee's hours per day.
  • Attendances move and resize by dragging, with Undo. An open attendance stays open when it is moved. The Community's own rules and errors apply.
  • A form of an attendance warns when the employee has no contract on its day, and an Employees officer follows a link to the employee to correct it.
  • Officers get two filters, Ongoing Contract and No Active Contract.
  • The worked-hours button of an employee opens a monthly Gantt view with the overview of the month above the grid.
  • With one empty row, the Attendances application's own helper shows over the grid. Uninstalling the module gives the actions back their list and calendar views.

Updating from the first version renames the view's identifier and keeps its database id. See the module README.rst and docs/modules/attendance_gantt.md.
