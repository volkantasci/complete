Attendances Timeline

The attendances of the employees on a timeline, one row per employee. The module installs by itself when the Attendances application and the Employees timeline module are both present, and adds no menu, group or setting.

  • Attendances > Management > Dashboard opens the Gantt view first, on the Day range of today, grouped by employee. One period is shown for each range.
  • Every employee has a row, also without attendances, with the avatar and the employee card, and a label and bar of the hours worked against the hours expected. The long form of the label shows from a window width of 1400 px and follows the window as it is resized.
  • Open attendances run up to now. Cells outside contracts and working hours are grey, columns nobody works fold, and a name is red when the employee has no contract in the period.
  • With one empty row, the Attendances application's own helper shows over the grid.
  • Uninstalling the module gives the action back its list and form views.

See the module README.rst and docs/modules/attendance_gantt.md.
