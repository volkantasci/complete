Employees Timeline

The bridge between the Employees application and the Gantt view. It installs by itself when both are present, and adds no menu, action, view, group or setting.

  • A variant of the Gantt view for rows that stand for employees: the avatar and the name in each row header, and the employee card on a click on the avatar.
  • A range chosen from the menu is built around today when today is on screen. Cells with two different halves are shaded along a diagonal, and a pill over both halves of a day is drawn on one half at lunch, on the half-day scale.
  • A server helper that answers the Gantt data call with every matching employee, those without records included, for the views that need it.
  • Other modules choose the variant in their view; it has nothing to open on its own.

See the module README.rst and docs/modules/employee_gantt.md.
