Time Off Timeline

The time off of the employees on a timeline, one row per employee and one pill per request. The module installs by itself when the Time Off application, Import and the Employees timeline module are present, and adds no menu, group or setting.

  • Time Off > Overview opens the leave view for Officers and Administrators, and the time-off report as a timeline for everyone else. Management > Time Off gets the Gantt view as its last view.
  • Pills show the duration, with the type's code for Officers. A request of one day or less reads its duration alone on the Month and Quarter ranges, whatever the search holds.
  • A search on employees keeps every matching employee, also without leave; a search on the requests keeps only the employees who have matching requests.
  • Approved leaves grey their days, also for flexible schedules. The attendance views take them into account too.
  • A label of hours on each row: hours to work less leave for a fixed schedule, hours of approved leave for a flexible one.
  • The employee card has a leave band; the gear offers Import and Export.
  • A click on a pill opens a popover with the request's details and the buttons its state and the Community's rules allow: Edit, Save, Approve, Refuse, Discard and Delete. A double click opens the request's dialog.
  • In the leave view pills move, resize and copy through the Community's own checks, with Undo; Second Approval and Approved pills are locked.
  • Officers and Administrators split a request at a day border and join the parts again with Undo.
  • On a phone the popover is the bottom sheet of the Gantt view.
  • Creating leaves from the cells follows in a later release; until then empty cells do nothing.
