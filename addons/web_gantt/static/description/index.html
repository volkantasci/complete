Gantt View

A timeline view for the web client. Records are drawn as bars on a time grid, in rows that can be grouped by any field. Records can be created, planned, opened, changed and deleted from the grid. Any window action can list it among its views.

  • Day, week, month, quarter and year ranges, a custom range, previous, next and Today.
  • Grouping on several levels, folding of group rows, one row per record in sparse mode, paging of groups.
  • Bars coloured, marked and filled by fields of the record, a popover with a summary, an optional total row.
  • Capacity bars, unavailable periods and folded columns, supplied by the modules that host the view.
  • Editing: a New button, creating from a clicked cell or a dragged selection, a plan dialog for records without dates, opening a record from the popover or by double click, deleting with confirmation, editable popover fields, and view options for creating, editing, deleting, planning, the form and a create action. Everything follows the rights of the user.
  • Sample data and empty states, phone layout, light and dark look.

Moving and resizing bars, dependencies and the HR and time-off views come in later releases.

See the module README.md for scope, provenance and tests.
