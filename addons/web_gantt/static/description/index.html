Gantt View

A timeline view for the web client. Records are drawn as bars on a time grid, in rows that can be grouped by any field. Records can be created, planned, opened, changed and deleted from the grid. Any window action can list it among its views.

  • Day, week, month, quarter and year ranges, a custom range, previous, next and Today.
  • Grouping on several levels, folding of group rows, one row per record in sparse mode, paging of groups.
  • Bars coloured, marked and filled by fields of the record, a popover with a summary, an optional total row.
  • Capacity bars, unavailable periods and folded columns, supplied by the modules that host the view.
  • Editing: a New button, creating from a clicked cell or a dragged selection, a plan dialog for records without dates, opening a record from the popover or by double click, deleting with confirmation, editable popover fields, and view options for creating, editing, deleting, planning, the form and a create action. Everything follows the rights of the user.
  • Moving and copying bars (with Ctrl) to another time or row, resizing them by their ends, time badges, edge scrolling, locked bars, a notification with Undo, whole days on date-only fields, touch and right-to-left support, and the view option to turn drag and drop off.
  • Selecting many cells (click, drawing, row and column headers with Ctrl and Shift), then adding one record per day and row from a template form, planning, or deleting them together; and a side panel of records without dates that can be dragged onto the grid, with a drop zone that removes the dates of a pill.
  • Dependencies: arrows between dependent bars, coloured by whether the order holds, with highlight; drawing a dependency from a bar's handles and removing one with the arrow's button; a switch to show or hide the arrows.
  • Rescheduling: moving or resizing a bar can also move the bars that follow, keeping or using the free time between them, in whole days for date-only records, with a notification and Undo, a warning for loops and for dates in the past, and a manual method that moves one bar only. Optional buffer bands show free time before and after a bar.
  • Sample data and empty states, phone layout, light and dark look.

The HR and time-off views come in later releases.
